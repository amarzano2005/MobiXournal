# MobiXournal v2.3.1 (Android) & Desktop v1.4.0 (Windows 11)

Released 2026-11-19 · 26 changes: the window rebuilt as one flat workstation, and the two fixes the tablet owed.

## The window, one surface at a time

- **One material.** A flat tonal ramp for both themes: panels are opaque, the sheet is the lightest thing in
  the window, and buttons, fields, menus, popups, floating bars and dialogs are all built on it.
- **One command hierarchy.** Primary, secondary, ghost and destructive buttons plus one field style, with the
  panel and inspector buttons migrated onto them — same height, same radius, same press.
- **A workstation bar.** The native title bar's caption blends into a flat header, the tab strip scrolls with
  the window's own tabs, and the status line no longer competes with either.
- **Docked tool strips.** The rail and the ribbon are bands of the window with hairline group dividers, a
  scroll cue and delayed tooltips, instead of capsules floating in lanes.
- **Menus and popups.** One flat menu without embossed chips or tiles, and every flyout — the colour panel
  included — shares one concentric shape.
- **Floating bars that float.** The selection bar and the PDF text bar are opaque surfaces, and the ring
  reads whole at its rim and at its clamped ends.
- **Dialogs.** Eleven modals became one frame: one scrim, one heading, one footer, one way to cancel.
- **Sidebar and inspector.** One styling for both lanes, the Inspector's footer in its honest order, and the
  sidebar split along its real responsibilities so neither file carries the other's subject.
- **The entry screen.** No halo glow, dividers on the workspace band, and a saving badge that stays on top
  of the sheet it reports on.
- **Settings.** The shell keeps a single nav indicator, a visible search field, flexible rows and clamped
  sliders, and the pages — Colors, Shortcuts, Ring, Stylus, Navigation, Toolbar, Audio and Appearance — were
  polished to one grammar per control.

## The window's Contents

- A notebook-owned row now offers **Move up** and **Move down** in its ⋯ menu, above Rename and Delete…, so a
  chapter can be reordered in the window the way the tablet already reordered it.

## The tablet

- **Restore last session brings a split run back whole.** A session closed with two documents side by side
  returns as both halves, each on the page it was left on — the button under *New sheet* and the workspace
  card now restore the same thing, and a leftover second-pane cache is not mistaken for a split.
- **A press in the air holds the selection.** A press inside the selection's own outline but on empty paper no
  longer drops it, so a stroke that starts in the gap of an L continues the move.

## Both shells

- A refused update check says it was refused, instead of reporting "already the latest", and the release feed
  is read without the API quota — a rate-limited service no longer looks like a quiet one.

## Behind the release

- A version is tagged only after the checks pass, a release title names both builds, and the publishing job is
  rehearsed without publishing; the four defects the 2.3.0 release hit are refused at freeze time.
