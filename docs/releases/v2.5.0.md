## Hybrid Spline & Living Elements (Both shells)

- **Hybrid scientific Spline**: added `SplineElement` with smooth Catmull-Rom interpolation and per-knot symmetric or broken Bézier handles across `.mobix`, `.xopp` synthesis, PDF/SVG export, Android, and Windows Desktop.
- **Direct spline editing**: add midpoint knots, toggle smooth/corner knots (circle vs square), remove knots, and open/close loops directly from the floating selection bar or inspector; Desktop now matches Mobile's compact icon draft pill, accent-coloured pins, and immediate knot/handle dragging.
- **Unified element interaction**: consistent Model 1 creation tools, proximity-only diamond connection pins (with circuit terminal snapping), polygonal hit-testing, and separate X/Y edge resize handles on aspect-locked figures and strokes.
- **Synchronized Desktop ink & styling**: Desktop `StrokeSlot`, Plot preview/placement, Table borders, `Line width` popup, and `shadeIntegral` polygon fill now stay synchronized with current pen colour, width, and line-style settings.

## Focus Mode, Chrome & Split View (Android)

- **Focus mode floating bar**: Liquid Glass quick-access floating bar (up to 10 customizable slots) with bottom/side edge docking, hold-and-drag customization, 3-zone touch double-tap across all tools, and a first-launch guide.
- **L-frame chrome & toolbar dividers**: separated the mobile document header and STEM dock with a seamless L-frame, removed docked bar capsules, added user-editable toolbar dividers across both shells, and unified Row 1 action plates.
- **Context-adaptive audio & LineStyle glyphs**: 3-mode audio controls (`Idle`, `Recording`, `Paused` with Play-triangle resume) on both shells, stroke-pattern glyphs on all 4 `LineStyle` chips, and a `Corner Hub` live tool indicator.
- **144 Hz transitions**: smooth chrome-inset Focus/sidebar animations, lag-free dual-side Split View sliding, zero-relayout search overlay, and symmetric Home-to-Editor transitions.

## Anchored Inspectors & Table Editing (Android)

- **Live anchored popups**: converted Shape, Plot, Circuit, and 2-tab Table editors into compact anchored popups with 4-side minimum-overlap placement, docked numeric/formula keyboards, and live canvas sync.
- **Table IME formula bar**: inline formula bar with arrow navigation and live active-cell canvas highlight.

## Performance, Search & Format (Both shells)

- **Pre-indexed handwriting search**: persistent `HandwritingLineCache` with batch concurrent ML Kit recognition.
- **Faster scroll, zoom & saves**: optimized `.xopp` / dense-ruling / PDF rendering, adjacent-page ink prefetch, uncompressed (`STORED`) pre-compressed media & PDF attachments in `.mobix` / `.xopp` ZIP archives, and unified **Embed PDF backgrounds** option across onboarding, settings, import, and Save As.
- **Auto-save & selection fixes**: audio stop triggers auto-save, `DrawingSurfaceView.load()` and closing the last tab clear stale selection popups on mobile, and `mobix:meta` revives cleanly on direct `.xopp` opens.
